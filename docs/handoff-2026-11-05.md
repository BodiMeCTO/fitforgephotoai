# Handoff, 2026-11-05

**Read this first.** What exists, what was measured, what was not done, and the first thing to do.

## In one paragraph
`fitforgephotoai` is a working prototype that builds a synthetic dataset of garments draped on
bodies with exact ease and elasticity labels and renders, driving the body model (annyextension)
and the solver (fitforgesolver) in-process. It was smoke-tested end to end on CPU and a 24-sample
demo was built. The 288-sample pilot was lost to a session restart (issue `2026-11-05-rest`).
The spec of what the three FitForge repos would need, and the decisions Keith has to take
(including that this reverses ADR-0080 decision 4), is `docs/spec-synthetic-ease-elasticity-dataset.md`.
No other repo was changed.

## What to do first
1. Rerun the pilot on a box that stays up: `scripts/run_workers.sh configs/pilot_cpu.yaml outputs/pilot_cpu 4`
   (about 5 h on 4 CPU cores; resumable). Then `report`, `shard`, `probe` (README "Run").
2. Put the spec's section 7 decisions to Keith as a briefing; section 8 is the recommended spike.

## Facts measured here (4-core cloud box, no GPU)
- Draft 1.3 s; drape 230 s with the solver's production settings at a 2 cm mesh (80 s with the
  plain defaults, but then the shirt slides off the shoulders); QC and labels under 2 s; four
  renders 5 s. About 60 samples an hour on 4 cores.
- Every drape ended at the 400-step ceiling unconverged (issue `2026-11-05-conv`).
- The solver's flat-pattern chart misreads waist and hip on a drafted shirt (issue `2026-11-05-chrt`);
  its mesh-measured ease is poisoned by the sleeves, so the builder has its own torso-only measure
  (`photoai/measure.py`, using the body model's tailor rule).
- The first smoke body (bust 113 cm) poked through the 2 cm cloth: QC fail, recorded as such.
