# 2026-11-05-chrt - the solver's flat-pattern chart reads waist and hip at the wrong level on a drafted shirt

- status: open
- severity: medium
- why: `fitforge.patterns.drafted_chart.chart_from_pattern` takes waist and hip girth 20 and 42 cm below the underarm (the PatternFast sidecar rule); on the fitted shirt drafted for a 162 cm body it gave waist 63 and hip 63 cm against a body of 106 and 112 (bust 116.7 was right). `ease_pattern_cm` is therefore trusted at the bust only.
- next: a solver change (spec S3): read the levels from the draft's own `waist_line` / `hips_line`; until then the label says bust only.
